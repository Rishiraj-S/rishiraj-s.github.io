The 5000-to-1 Miracle: How Leicester City Won the Premier League. A Data-Driven Tribute to Jamie Vardy and Co.

In this project, I used Python and StatsBomb event data to analyze Leicester City’s 2015–16 Premier League title-winning season. I explored tactical formations, possession trends, shot and xG patterns, and key player performances, especially Jamie Vardy. Visualizations and data storytelling reveal how Leicester defied the odds to make history.

Stack
Python, pandas, mplsoccer, matplotlib, seaborn, statsbombpy, Jupyter
